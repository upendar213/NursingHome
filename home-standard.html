HELPFUL ANSWERS

A Little Clarity
Before You Begin.

Find answers about the services and information shown on this website.

What services can I explore?

The website presents elder care, patient-care support, post-surgery assistance, and scheduled nursing visits. Visit the Services page for details.

Is this a live home-care agency website?

No. Meridian is a demonstration project. This website does not arrange caregivers, accept payments, or confirm care services.

Can care duration be personalized?

A real agency would need to review requirements, availability, and terms before confirming a personalized arrangement.

Does the Help Form create a booking?

No. The current Help Form is a demonstration. Messages are not sent or saved, and no booking or reply is provided.
