COMMON QUESTIONS

Pricing Questions, Answered

A few details about the example plans shown above.

Are these actual agency prices?

No. All prices and package terms are fictional examples created for this project. Replace them with approved agency information before publishing.

Are medicines and dressing materials included?

In this sample pricing model, medicines, dressing materials, and medical equipment are excluded. Any additional costs would need to be confirmed before booking.

Can I request a different care duration?

Use the care enquiry page to describe your preferred schedule and requirements for a personalized quote.

Does personal care include injections or dressing?

No. The example personal-care packages are separate from nursing procedures. Nursing requirements must be reviewed and arranged separately.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Payment, rescheduling, and cancellation policies have not been defined for this demonstration. Approved terms should be provided before accepting a real booking.
