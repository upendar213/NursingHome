01 Post-Surgery Care
RECOVERY SUPPORT

Recovery Support in the Comfort of Home

Explore assistance with everyday activities during recovery after hospital discharge or surgery. Support can be considered around personal routines and the instructions provided by the treating team.

Support options

  • Daily Routine Assistance

    Help with everyday activities, personal comfort, and familiar routines.

  • Medication Reminders

    Reminders according to the supplied prescription and agreed care arrangement.

  • Mobility Assistance

    Assistance with movement within the limits of the patient’s care instructions.

  • Family Updates

    Everyday observations and agreed care updates shared with the family.

Personal-care support is separate from clinical nursing procedures. Post-operative and wound-care requirements must follow the treating team’s instructions.

02 Elder Care
EVERYDAY ELDER SUPPORT

Comfort, Companionship & Everyday Assistance

Explore support options for older adults who need assistance with personal routines, companionship, or everyday activities at home.

Support options

  • Companionship

    Conversation, company, and engagement around personal interests.

  • Daily Living Assistance

    Support with everyday routines and agreed personal activities.

  • Personal Hygiene Support

    Respectful assistance with personal care, privacy, and everyday comfort.

  • Mobility Assistance

    Help with walking and movement according to the agreed support requirements.

Care duration and assistance levels would need individual review. Nursing procedures are not automatically included in personal-care support.

03 Injections & Dressing
SCHEDULED NURSING VISITS

Scheduled Nursing Support at Home

Explore scheduled visits for prescribed injections and wound-dressing requirements, subject to appropriate clinical review and care instructions.

Visit options

  • Prescribed Injection Visits

    An agreed injection service following prescription review and suitability checks.

  • Wound Dressing

    Dressing care according to the relevant clinical instructions.

  • Dressing Changes

    Dressing or bandage changes when indicated by the care plan.

  • Visit Documentation

    Documentation of the agreed service and relevant care observations.

Clinical procedures require appropriately qualified professionals and suitable instructions. Medicines and dressing supplies are separate in this sample pricing model.

04 Patient Care Support
PERSONAL CARE ASSISTANCE

Personal Support for Everyday Comfort

Explore assistance for patients who need extra help with personal care and daily activities at home, with attention to dignity and familiar routines.

Support options

  • Personal Care Assistance

    Help with agreed daily activities and individual comfort needs.

  • Hygiene Support

    Respectful assistance with personal cleanliness and everyday routines.

  • Meal Assistance

    Meal setup and appropriate eating assistance within the agreed care plan.

  • Mobility and Positioning Support

    Assistance according to assessed needs and suitable care instructions.

Clinical procedures are not automatically included. Support requirements and any specialist needs would require individual review.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A Little Clarity Before You Begin

Learn about the service categories and demonstration information on this website.

What is the difference between personal care and nursing visits?

The personal-care categories on this website focus on daily routines and practical assistance. Nursing visits describe separate clinical services requiring appropriate qualifications, review, and instructions.

Are medicines and dressing supplies included?

No. In this demonstration pricing model, medicines, dressing supplies, and medical equipment are not included. See the Pricing page for the example package terms.

Can I explore a different care duration?

The Pricing page presents several example durations. A live agency would need to review requirements, availability, and terms before confirming a personalized arrangement.

Does submitting the Help Form confirm a booking?

No. The Help Form is a demonstration only. Messages are not sent or saved, and no booking, availability confirmation, or reply is provided.
