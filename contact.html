COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Make an Enquiry

A few details about using this project’s enquiry form.

What if I am unsure which service to choose?

Select “Not Sure” in the Care Service field and briefly describe the type of assistance you are exploring.

Can I enquire for a family member?

Yes. Choose Parent, Relative, or Other in the Enquiring For field. Avoid sharing their sensitive personal or medical information.

Does submitting this form confirm a booking?

No. This is a demonstration form. It does not send an enquiry, reserve a caregiver, or confirm a service.

Should I upload medical documents?

No. This form does not accept document uploads. Please do not paste medical records into the message field.

How will I receive a response?

This demonstration does not collect a reply method or send responses. A live enquiry service would require a suitable response channel and backend integration.
